## How to read this ledger

- **Horizon** runs 1s → 60s → 2m → 5m → 10m → 15m → 30m. The genuine, deriv-tradeable EURUSD direction edge is **15m compression×NY ≈ 0.647 combined** (only horizon at/above deriv's 15-minute forex Rise/Fall floor). Everything < 15m is research-only (synthetic-indices-only on deriv).
- **Honest result = deriv-faithful**: non-overlapping trades (chronological de-overlap, no look-ahead), ties LOSE, mid-to-mid no spread, entry = next tick (1s lag), bootstrap CI95, select-on-VAL → verify each of 2024/2025/2026. Breakeven at R≈1.85 payout = **0.541**.
- **CRITICAL — pre-audit inflation**: the 2026-05-30/31 BIAS AUDIT found the headline tick/1m/2m numbers (0.78–0.87, 0.764, 0.756–0.814) were **inflated** by (a) bar-shift horizon labels — a "60-bar" "1-min" trade spanned a MEDIAN of 111 wall-clock seconds — and (b) greedy de-overlap (peeking ahead to keep the most-confident bar per cluster, +3 to +9 acc pts) and (c) best-of-search multiple-testing (corr(VALacc,OOSacc)=−0.54). The `honest result` column records the **CORRECTED** deriv-faithful number; §3 lists the headline-vs-corrected pairs.
- **Status vocab**: `helped` (added genuine lift / mechanistic finding) · `null` (no edge) · `best` (best honest result of its session/class) · `deliverable` (frozen production book or shipped artifact) · `tooling` (infra/audit/data-build) · `superseded` (pre-audit artifact or replaced by a later corrected run).
- **The one durable positive**: DIRECTION is ~EMH (≈0.51–0.52 AUC) at every horizon ≥ 60s across every model class; **MAGNITUDE (|return|) is strongly forecastable OOS** (large-move AUC 0.71–0.81 at 10m/30m, 0.68 at 60s/120s) — tradeable on Touch/Straddle/Range, NOT up/down. This is the sign-invariance theorem (arXiv:2512.15720) confirmed on EURUSD.
