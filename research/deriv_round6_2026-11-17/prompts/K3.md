## The program

Goal: the best demonstrable out-of-sample predictor of the 15-minute (also 30-minute) direction of seven FX pairs as
quoted by the broker Deriv (AUDUSD, EURUSD, GBPUSD, NZDUSD, USDCAD, USDCHF, USDJPY) for its Rise/Fall binary
contract. UP and DOWN are separate keys. Settlement: entry = first tick at or after decision + 1 s, exit = last tick
at or before entry + H; ties lose; breakeven win rate 0.541. Decisions are made at the close of every one-minute bar.
Certification: per-fold-refit combinatorial purged cross-validation (6 chronological groups, all 15 two-group test
paths, purge and embargo of one horizon), New York 08:00-17:00 only. A side passes iff at least 12 of the 15 paths have
>= 25 trades and a win rate >= 0.541, the 10th percentile of path win rates is >= 0.541, and the pair's moved up-rate
is within [0.47, 0.53] (a drifting pair is instead judged against an hour x volatility matched always-side placebo).
Trades are the most confident validation-quantile scores per side (default: top 3%), first-come de-overlapped by one
horizon. A passing side then needs survivor checks and ONE look at a sealed 3.5-month holdout.

Data (the only data in use): Deriv 1-second mid ticks for the seven pairs, development window 2025-08-11 ..
2026-07-31 (about 354,000 one-minute decisions per pair, about 129,000 in session; 15-minute labels overlap 15x, so
roughly 8,600 non-overlapping outcomes per pair). Deriv serves only about 365 days of history, so the sample cannot be
extended backwards. Horizons below 15 minutes are not tradable targets (features may use sub-minute information).

Hardware now available: 20 CPU threads, 62 GB RAM and an idle NVIDIA RTX 5050 (8 GB) with PyTorch 2.12 CUDA,
XGBoost CUDA and CatBoost GPU working. Installed: lightgbm 4.6, xgboost 3.2, catboost 1.2, scikit-learn 1.8, torch,
pytorch_tabnet, sktime, darts, chronos 2.2, river, hmmlearn, arch, pywt, statsmodels, numba, polars, optuna.
